** Actual quilt patterns
** Value contrast control
*** Deliberately bias light colors to one region and dark colors to another (top/bottom, center/edge). Currently palette colors are assigned randomly — intentional value distribution could improve composition.
* Designs
** Quilt-level color wash
*** Shift which palette colors are chosen based on each block's position — warm colors cluster in one corner, cool in another. Operates at composition level rather than within individual blocks.
** Asymmetric grid
*** Vary row heights or column widths — creates a "strippy quilt" feel very different from the current uniform grid.
** Bargello columns
*** Not a block pattern but a new layout mode: vertical strips of varying height that shift up/down to create an undulating wave across the whole quilt. Classic technique, very striking.
** Appliqué block
*** Circle or leaf shape layered on a background square. Approximated with many-sided polygons. Adds organic rounded forms that no current block has.
** Sashiko overlay
*** Post-render texture pass that draws running-stitch patterns (waves, hemp leaf, concentric circles) in white/cream thread over finished blocks. Purely decorative, like real quilting stitch patterns. Different from quilt_stitch which just does grid lines.
** Improv/wonky mode
*** A chaos modifier that jitters vertex positions on any block by a small random amount. Makes everything slightly off-kilter, mimicking modern improv quilting. Global param rather than a new block.
** Cathedral windows
*** Overlapping folded-fabric circles creating diamond-shaped reveals of a contrast color. Very distinctive look.
